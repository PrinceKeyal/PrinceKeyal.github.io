Architecture

One pipeline from the field to the boardroom.

Sales agents, delivery vans, billing counters and cold stores each produce data in their own system. My work connects them, cleans the data, adds intelligence on top and delivers it to management.

Sources include MIS and billing software, the GPS fleet management system, the delivery app and agent sales records.

The core is Python services, SQL and REST APIs, with LangChain and Claude AI pipelines for analysis and document processing, and TensorFlow and PyTorch models for forecasting.

Outputs are the daily CEO Dashboard, stock and procurement reports, and the operational trackers teams work from.
